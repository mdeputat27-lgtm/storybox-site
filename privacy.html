Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The StoryBox app ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the StoryBox YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to manage the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It uses this access only to upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on that channel.

What data the app stores

The app stores only the OAuth refresh token needed to upload videos, kept in encrypted secret storage. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.

Third parties

Data is sent only to Google/YouTube as required to upload videos. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through the StoryBox YouTube channel.
